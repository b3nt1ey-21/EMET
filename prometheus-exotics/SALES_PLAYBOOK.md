# Prometheus Exotics — Sales Playbook 🏎️

Your goal: **drive booked rentals** and earn your **10% commission** on each one.
You are a lead-generation partner. You don't need to own cars, handle insurance, or
deliver — you just need to put the right *renter* in front of the company with **your
referral code attached** so you get credited.

This playbook is your operating system. Work it daily.

---

## 0. Set up so you actually get paid (do this first)

1. Open `script.js` and edit the `CONFIG` block:
   - `referralCode` — a code the company recognizes as **you** (e.g. `PROM-BINO`).
   - `agentName` — your name.
   - `companyPhone` / `companyEmail` — the company's booking line.
2. **Confirm the tracking with the owner.** Get them to agree *in writing* (a text is fine)
   that any lead carrying your code = your 10%. This single conversation protects every
   future commission. Suggested message:

   > "Hey — I'm sending you booked renters. Every lead I send will say `Referral: PROM-BINO`
   > at the top. Can we lock in that anything with that tag is my 10%? Want to make sure we
   > both track it clean."

3. Deploy the page (see `README.md`) and use **that link everywhere**. The form stamps your
   code onto every request automatically.

> 💡 Even better if the owner gives you a unique discount code for *customers* (e.g. "10% off
> with code BINO"). Customers love a deal, and it becomes undeniable proof the booking was yours.

## 2. The daily routine (≈30–45 min) — "10 touches a day"

Consistency beats intensity. Every day:

- [ ] **3 posts/stories** — at least one short video (Reels/TikTok). See content calendar below.
- [ ] **5 DMs** — to event planners, photographers, or warm prospects (scripts below).
- [ ] **2 comments** — add value on OC lifestyle / car / event posts (then they check your profile).
- [ ] **Reply to every inquiry within 5 minutes.** Speed is the #1 thing that wins exotic bookings.

> Booking math: most exotic rentals here run **$400–$1,500/day**. On a 2-day Lambo weekend
> (~$2,600), your 10% ≈ **$260** from one booking. **Five bookings a month ≈ $1,000–$1,500.**
> You need *volume of conversations*, not luck.

## 3. Outreach scripts (copy, tweak, send)

### A. Event planners / wedding planners (partnership — highest leverage)
> Hi [Name]! I work with Prometheus Exotics — we do exotic & luxury car rentals across OC
> (Lambos, Ferraris, Rolls-Royce, G-Wagons). A lot of your couples would love a grand exit or
> a photo car. I'd love to be your go-to for that — I can offer your clients a special rate and
> make it totally hands-off for you. Open to a quick chat?

### B. Photographers / content creators
> Hey [Name], love your work — especially the [specific shoot]. I rent exotics in OC and a hero
> car would level up shoots like that. Want to collab? I can get you a car at a creator rate for
> your next session, and you tag us. Could be a great look for both of us.

### C. Warm DM to someone posting about a birthday / trip / milestone
> Happy early birthday! 🎉 If you want to make the weekend unforgettable, I do exotic car rentals
> in OC — we deliver straight to you. Could roll up in a Lambo or a Rolls for the day. Want me to
> send options + pricing? No pressure.

### D. Hotel / Airbnb concierge (recurring referral source)
> Hi [Name], do your guests ever ask about renting something special to drive while they're here?
> I run exotic car rentals in OC with delivery to your property. Happy to set up a referral
> arrangement so your guests get a premium add-on and you get taken care of. Worth a quick call?

### E. Fast reply to an inbound "how much?" (don't just drop a number)
> Great choice! The [car] is [$X]/day, and we deliver free anywhere in OC on 2+ day rentals.
> What dates are you thinking, and where would you want it delivered? I'll lock in availability
> for you right now. 🔑

**Rules:** personalize the first line, ask a question at the end, follow up once after 2–3 days
("Still want me to hold that car for your dates?"), and **always** push them to the booking link.

## 6. Close more: objection handling

- **"It's expensive."** → "Totally fair — but for [their occasion] it's one day you'll never
  forget, and it's cheaper than you'd think split across the group. Want the 2-day rate? It's
  the better deal."
- **"I'm not sure of dates."** → "No problem — tell me the rough weekend and I'll hold a car
  with no commitment so you don't lose it."
- **"Let me think about it."** → "Of course. These book out fast on weekends — want me to pencil
  you in so it's there if you decide? Easy to release."
- **Ghosted?** → One friendly follow-up after 2–3 days, then let it rest. Move to the next lead.
