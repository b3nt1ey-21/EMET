Good to know

Questions, answered

How old do I need to be to rent?

Most of our fleet is available to drivers 21+. Select supercars may require 25+. A valid license and proof of insurance are required.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we deliver and pick up anywhere in Orange County. Delivery is complimentary on 2+ day rentals within 25 miles of our hub.

What's included?

Every rental includes a set mileage allowance, full detail, and a full tank. Extra miles and optional insurance are available — all priced up front, no surprises.

How do I pay?

We accept major cards and digital payment. A refundable security hold applies during your rental period.
